# Portada (S01)

Scope: landing at `/` (Persuade), sharing its world with `/precios` (Persuade) and, with restraint, the app screens (Operate).
Audience: 3D-printing makers with a photo or an idea who want a printable file. Job: understand that one photo becomes a
downloadable model, trust the price, upload. Proof: the real interface and 4–6 real models generated with the production
engine (labelled as examples made with My3DStudio). No testimonials, counts or logos exist.
Memorable moment: the model on the page is "painted" step by step, grey primer to finished, as the visitor moves through the steps.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Generating a model reads like painting a miniature: photo, grey primer mesh, finished texture, printable file, with the price on the pot lid. It refuses the category default of a glowing 3D render floating on black under a centred headline.

OWN-WORLD: Matte primer grey owns the ground in large fields; bone paper for guide pages; near-black ink for type and keylines. The component language is the paint pot: a round saturated lid (red, blue, ochre, green) with a printed label, used for steps, plans and packs. One strict 8px module; leader-line labels name model facts in tabular figures (mm, polygons, credits). A condensed display face with the voice of a painting guide over a plain working sans.

STORY: The maker sees a real printed-style miniature, understands photo → model → file, believes the price because it is on the lid before anything runs, and drops a photo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large real model in the live viewer on a primer-grey field, turning with inertia on a round base; four numbered pots in a row beside it (Foto, Malla gris, Textura, Archivo); headline top left at display scale; the first pot is the working drop zone with Generar · 50 créditos inside it.

FORM: La guía de pintado, candidate 4 of my ordered list of 7, seed:211effcb2f1b9ebf. Signature interaction: choosing a pot repaints the live model (photo plate, grey primer, texture, file with leader-line measurements). Motion grammar: inertial orbit, pots press like lids, one orchestrated entrance.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
